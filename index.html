How Janasakthi works for you

Talk to your leader directly

Select your constituency – send a message or voice note to your MLA, district panchayat president, or CM. Get a unique tracking ID.

Track every complaint

Your grievance moves through the system: Received → Assigned to volunteer → Escalated to department → Resolved. You get SMS/WhatsApp updates.

Rate your representative

After resolution, rate your MLA/minister privately. Leadership uses this to improve performance – and you remain anonymous.

Promise tracker

See what your local candidate promised before election – and what has been delivered. Complete public transparency.

Scheme Sahayak – Your Personal Scheme Assistant

No more wondering which government scheme you qualify for. Janasakthi’s AI engine scans your family profile (income, occupation, land, etc.) and shows you exactly which central & state schemes you can benefit from – with direct application link.

Volunteer Management Tree – How your issue reaches the right person

Janasakthi is powered by a structured volunteer network that ensures no citizen is left unattended. Every family is linked to a local volunteer, and every grievance automatically climbs the hierarchy if unresolved.

Booth VolunteerAssigned to 50 families. Collects issues, updates family data, forwards urgent matters.
Ward PresidentManages 5-10 booths. Monitors pending complaints, coordinates with local body.
Block PresidentOversees 20-30 wards. Reviews unresolved grievances, escalates to MLA.
District PresidentAggregates data for all blocks. Flags systemic issues to district administration.
State LeadershipMLAs, Ministers, and CM see dashboards – real‑time status of every pending grievance across Kerala.

45,000+ trained volunteers | Each volunteer gets a mobile app to manage families, submit reports, and receive alerts.

Speech & Debate Assistant

All speeches by your leader in Assembly/Parliament are logged. AI fact‑checks opposition claims in real time. Citizens can see “promises made vs done”.

Family Data & Scheme Eligibility

Each family’s employment, education, and scheme status is updated by the assigned volunteer – helping leaders target welfare accurately.
